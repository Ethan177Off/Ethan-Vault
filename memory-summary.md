## Current Market Signals

### Cycling Glove Market

Current evidence suggests the Taiwan cycling glove market is fragmented.

No single glove brand appears to dominate the entire category.

This creates room for differentiation, but also means retailers and customers already have many alternatives.

### Price Signal

Approximately NT$590 appears to be an important existing retail price band in at least one relevant cycling retail environment.

Treat this as a market signal, not a universal pricing rule.

### Retail Selling Behavior

Retail staff may not proactively promote gloves strongly.

Therefore product quality alone is unlikely to be sufficient.

CHIBA needs simple and clear sales logic that makes recommendation easier.

## Important Operating Principle

Do not scale complexity faster than validated demand.

Prefer:

Opportunity
→ Pilot
→ Measure
→ Learn
→ Adjust
→ Scale

For reversible decisions, prioritize speed and testing.

For inventory, contracts, large financial commitments, strategic partnerships, scientific claims, or difficult-to-reverse decisions, increase verification rigor.
