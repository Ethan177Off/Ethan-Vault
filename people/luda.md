## Current Interpretation

The NT$590 observation is useful evidence from 路達's own retail environment.

It should NOT yet be treated as:

> The optimal glove price for the entire Taiwan market.

Current implications:

- A mainstream CHIBA Hero SKU should be tested near realistic existing market price bands.
- Premium products require a clearer reason to trade up.
- Retail sales tools may materially affect sell-through because gloves are not always actively recommended by staff.
- Product selection should be simple enough for staff to explain quickly.

## Guardrails

Do not commit without review to:

- Long-term exclusivity
- Permanent special pricing
- Excessive early inventory
- Unlimited returns
- Structurally weak margin
- Unclear payment terms
- Large marketing subsidies

Preserve flexibility during early validation.
