# CHIBA Taiwan Channel Development — Decisions

> Purpose: Record project-level decisions and their reasoning.
> Major durable decisions may later be promoted into `/memory`.

## 2026-09 — Validate Before Scaling

### Decision

Do not pursue rapid store-count expansion before proving sell-through and reorder behavior.

### Reason

Partner interest alone does not establish channel-market fit.

### Implication

Prioritize:

Pilot
→ Sell-through
→ Reorder
→ Scale

## 2026-09 — Focus Hero SKUs

### Decision

Use a limited number of Hero SKU roles rather than promoting the full catalog equally.

### Current Candidate Roles

- Mainstream road cycling
- Winter / wind protection
- Women's short-finger

### Reason

A smaller focused assortment simplifies:

- Retail staff training
- Consumer choice
- Inventory management
- Marketing communication
- Sales analysis

### Status

Working decision.

Final SKU selection still requires validation.

---

## 2026-09 — Use Good-Better-Best Architecture

### Decision

Use Good-Better-Best as a working retail presentation structure where appropriate.

### Reason

The model can help:

- Simplify choice
- Establish product hierarchy
- Create price anchors
- Help staff recommend products
- Prevent every SKU from competing on the same logic

### Constraint

Do not force a product into a tier if the market evidence does not support it.

---

## 2026-09 — Retail Sales Tools Are Part of the Product Strategy

### Decision

Treat sales enablement materials as part of channel execution rather than optional marketing decoration.

### Initial Tools

- POP
- Pain-point messaging
- Product benefit cards
- Staff talking points
- Product comparisons

### Reason

Retail staff may not proactively explain glove differences without support.

---

## 2026-09 — Preserve Early Commercial Flexibility

### Decision

Avoid unnecessarily rigid long-term commitments during early channel validation.

### Applies To

- Large MOQ
- Excessive inventory
- Long exclusivity
- Weak payment terms
- Unfavorable return structures
- Difficult-to-reverse commercial commitments

### Reason

The channel model is still being validated.
